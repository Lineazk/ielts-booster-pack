IELTS Academic Writing band 9 Masterclass

Unlock structured writing templates and essential academic phrases designed to capture high scoring marks for Task 1 and Task 2.

Task 1: Academic Graph / Chart Structure

1. Introduction (1 Sentence): Paraphrase the graph prompt.
"The bar chart illustrates the proliferation of digital devices across three metropolitan zones from 2020 to 2025."
2. Overview (2 Sentences): State main trends without numbers.
"Overall, it is manifest that while mobile devices experienced rapid expansion, standard desk terminals became increasingly obsolete."
3. Detailed Paragraphs (2 Paragraphs): Cite specific values.
"Specifically, phone ownership reached its zenith in 2024, hovering at exactly eighty percent..."
illustrated by reached its zenith manifest profound disparity

Task 2: Academic Essay Band 9 Checklist

1. Introduction: Hook + Paraphrase + Clear Thesis.
"It is often asserted that digital platforms have a deleterious impact on public communication. I deeply agree with this paradigm..."
2. Body Paragraph 1 (Topic + Example):
"The most salubrious advantage of this transition is information access. For instance, online classrooms..."
3. Body Paragraph 2 (Counter/Detail):
"However, the pernicious effects of algorithmic feeds must not be ignored, as they exacerbate social divisions..."
4. Conclusion: Rephrase thesis + final warning.
"In conclusion, to mitigate these consequences, authorities must enforce pragmatic regulation..."
deleterious pernicious salubrious mitigate
